## How much does a Bloomberg Terminal cost per month?

About **$2,000 to $2,665 a month** for one user, on a contract that lasts far longer than a month. The fine print:

- **Contract:** Godel Discount says the minimum is typically two years, with a 90-day notice clause to cancel.
- **Set-up:** a one-time $500 to $1,500 for each terminal (Vendr).
- **Extras:** a second screen is $500 to $1,000. A replacement keyboard is about $300. Extra data packages, called add-ons, average about $1,200 (all Godel Discount).

**Plan on $24,500 to $35,500 for year one.** The low end is Vendr's $24,000 plus its lowest set-up fee, $500. The high end is Godel Discount's own year-one total.

## Why is a Bloomberg Terminal so expensive?

A Terminal is a whole trading workplace, and you pay for all of it. Godel Discount lists what the money buys:

- **Prices:** live quotes from 350+ exchanges.
- **News:** Bloomberg's own newsroom.
- **Research:** Bloomberg Intelligence, in-house reports on 2,000+ companies.
- **Chat:** the network where traders, bond traders especially, do their deals. Godel Discount says the network is the product.

A trader uses all of it every day. A long-term investor uses a small slice and still pays for the whole. Godel Discount adds that critics trace the price rises to Bloomberg's pricing power.

### Can one person buy a Bloomberg Terminal?

Yes, at the full one-terminal rate: $31,980 a year in Godel Discount's estimate. Every price above is for one terminal and one user.

### How much does a Bloomberg Terminal cost for a team of 10?

About $220,000 to $283,000 a year, before set-up and add-ons. Vendr puts a team that size at $22,000 to $25,000 a terminal. Godel Discount says $28,320.

### Can you rent a Bloomberg Terminal by the month?

Not really. Godel Discount says the minimum is typically two years, and that you cannot sign up for a single month and cancel. Ask Bloomberg for its current terms before you sign.

### Can I use a Bloomberg Terminal for free?

Not on your own. Vendr says universities pay about $2,000 to $5,000 a terminal a year, a fraction of the price above, so ask your university library whether it has one. For free stock tools, see [Bloomberg Terminal alternatives, free and cheap, compared](/alternatives/bloomberg-terminal/).
