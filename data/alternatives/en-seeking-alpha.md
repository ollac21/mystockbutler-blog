# Is Seeking Alpha Premium worth it? What you get for one stock

Seeking Alpha Premium is worth it if you read opinions on many stocks every week: for $299 a year you get articles by many contributors, quant ratings and AI summaries. It is not worth it if you research a few companies a year and want the company's own numbers checked: then paying per report costs less. Here is what each option gives you, and the arithmetic.

## What Seeking Alpha Premium costs and gives you

Prices as shown on Seeking Alpha's pricing page on 26 October 2026.

- **Price:** $4.95 for the first month, then $299 a year. The pricing page shows no monthly plan. Seeking Alpha PRO costs $2,400 a year; the stock-picking service Alpha Picks is sold separately.
- **What you get:** articles written by contributors, quant ratings built on five factors (valuation, growth, profitability, momentum and earnings revisions), AI summaries, screeners.
- **What it is good at:** many opinions, fast, on almost any US stock, and a lively comment section.
- **What to keep in mind:** an article is one contributor's view. Check the key numbers against the company's own filings before you rely on them.

## The arithmetic: a subscription or a report per stock?

If you research **a few stocks a year**, paying per report wins. At MyStockButler, a report already in our library costs $5 and a new one $20; the first one is free, and every account gets 5 free credits a month, enough for one library report. Ten reports from our library cost $50, a sixth of the yearly subscription.

If you read about **dozens of stocks a year**, a subscription wins. Seeking Alpha Premium's $299 a year is the price of about 60 library reports or 15 new ones, and it covers as many stocks as you want to read about.

## Questions

### Is Seeking Alpha Premium worth the money?

Yes, if you read about many stocks every week and value contributors' opinions. If you research a few companies a year, a free tool or a report per stock costs less.

### Does Seeking Alpha have a monthly plan?

Not on its pricing page as of 26 October 2026: Premium is $4.95 for the first month, then $299 billed yearly. Older reviews mention a monthly price; check the page before you pay.

### What is a free alternative to Seeking Alpha?

Stock Analysis and Yahoo Finance give financial statements, ratios and news for free. Simply Wall St gives 5 company reports a month for free. MyStockButler's first full report is free.

### Can I rely on the numbers in Seeking Alpha articles?

Each article is its contributor's analysis and opinion. Check the key numbers against the company's own filings before you rely on them.
