# Is Seeking Alpha Premium worth it? What you get for one stock

Seeking Alpha Premium is worth it if you read opinions on many stocks every week: for $299 a year you get articles by many contributors, quant ratings and AI summaries. It is not worth it if you research a few companies a year and want the company's own numbers checked: then paying per report costs less. Here is what each option gives you, and the arithmetic.

## What Seeking Alpha Premium costs and gives you

Prices as shown on Seeking Alpha's pricing page on 26 October 2026.

- **Price:** $4.95 for the first month, then $299 a year. The pricing page shows no monthly plan. Seeking Alpha PRO costs $2,400 a year; the stock-picking service Alpha Picks is sold separately.
- **What you get:** articles written by contributors, quant ratings built on five factors (valuation, growth, profitability, momentum and earnings revisions), AI summaries, screeners.
- **What it is good at:** many opinions, fast, on almost any US stock, and a lively comment section.
- **What to keep in mind:** an article is one contributor's view. Check the key numbers against the company's own filings before you rely on them.
