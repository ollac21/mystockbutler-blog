## How much does Koyfin cost?

Prices as shown on Koyfin's pricing page on 2 November 2026.

- **Free:** $0. 2 years of financials, 1 year of estimates, 2 watchlists, 2 screens, 2 custom dashboards.
- **Plus:** $39 a month. 10 years of financials and 10 years of estimates, unlimited watchlists and screens, a stock and ETF screener, "100K+ global company snapshots", and press releases, filings, transcripts and premium news.
- **Premium:** $79 a month. Everything in Plus, plus advanced portfolio analytics, unlimited custom data and formulas, and ETF valuation.
- **Advisor Core and Advisor Pro:** $209 and $299 a month. Client proposals and reports, integrations. Built for advisors.
- **Teams:** price on request.
- **Billing:** the page says an annual plan saves "up to 30%" and shows no annual price. Plans renew on their own. It describes a 100% satisfaction guarantee, with refunds asked for within 30 days.

**What Koyfin does well:** a lot of screen for the money. You get charts, dashboards, a deep screener, and transcripts you can search for a word.

**What it leaves to you:** everything after the data. On the pages we read, Koyfin shows data, charts, news and transcripts. We found no written report on a single company. You read the filings, you read the calls, you decide which numbers matter.

## What do you get after the screen?

A terminal answers the question you thought to ask. A report answers the ones you didn't. Here is the difference on real reports.

### "VICI's cash earnings are up. Is the dividend safe?"

Our [VICI report](/stocks/vici/) has the numbers a terminal has: VICI earned $2.38 of AFFO a share in 2025, up from $2.26, and paid out 74.2% of it. Then it keeps going. Caesars and MGM supply about 74% of VICI's leasing revenue. The bear case, at 27% probability, is Caesars' weaker credit turning into a cash problem, and it puts the shares at $20.17 by October 2027, against $23.52 on 29 October 2026.

That last part is what you would otherwise piece together yourself, from the filings.

### "Did management do what it said?"

Where management gives forecasts, we check them against results and score the record out of 5. VICI scores 4 of 5: its total AFFO beat management's range in all 5 years, and per share it beat the range in 4 and met it in 1. Koyfin Plus gives you the filings and the transcripts to dig this out yourself. The report has already dug.

### "The CEO sold shares. Should I worry?"

Each report comes with a research hub: management bios, interview links, the last 8 earnings-call transcripts, and a table of insider filings. Amazon's shows 299 filings between October 2025 and October 2026, 129 of them open-market sales. The report tells you which are real selling and which are pay mechanics. See [Amazon](/stocks/amzn/).

### "Results came out this morning. Is it already out of date?"

The 100 largest S&P 500 companies and the day's most talked-about stocks are redone first when they report, usually within about 5 hours.

## Questions

### Is Koyfin free?

Partly. The free plan gives you 2 years of financials, 1 year of estimates, 2 watchlists and 2 screens. Transcripts and filings start with Plus, at $39 a month. Premium costs $79 a month.

### Is Koyfin worth it?

For screening and charting, yes: Plus is $39 a month and covers 10 years of financials and estimates. For a decision on one company, you still do the reading. A report that traces each number to its source does that reading for you.

### Does Koyfin have earnings call transcripts?

Yes, from the Plus plan, which lists "press releases, filings & transcripts". MyStockButler's research hub holds the last 8 earnings-call transcripts for a covered company, next to the report that checks what management promised.

### What is a good alternative to Koyfin?

For global financials and a valuation model, TIKR. For deep screens and portfolio tracking, Stock Rover. For full written reports with every number traced to its source, MyStockButler (our product): your first S&P 500 report is free, and $30 a month opens every S&P 500 report.

### How do I know MyStockButler's numbers are right?

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed. A final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI models write the reports, so one can still get a number wrong: check the numbers you rely on.

### Is MyStockButler investment advice?

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
