## How much does Kavout cost?

From Kavout's pricing page, read on 2 November 2026. The page has a monthly and a yearly switch, and yearly billing takes 20% off.

- **Free, $0:** 10 research credits a month, US stocks and ETFs, crypto and forex, one AI research agent and a limited set of financial metrics.
- **Pro, $16 a month billed yearly ($192 every 12 months):** 1,000 research credits a month, 7 AI research agents, the AI Stock Picker, Smart Signals, live market movers, real-time alerts, and global stocks and ETFs. Its sign-up button reads "Get Pro, $20/mo".

## What do you get with Kavout?

- **A Kai Score.** The AI Stock Picker gives each stock a Kai Score from 1 to 9. Kavout describes it as a composite of technicals, fundamentals and market sentiment. From 6 to 9 reads Outperform, 3 to 5.9 Neutral, 1 to 2.9 Underperform. Thirteen strategies pick stocks, and the rankings are recalculated every day after the market closes.
- **Signals.** Smart Signals shows entry and exit signals across stocks, ETFs, crypto and forex.
- **Agents and questions.** InvestGPT answers questions about a stock. Pro adds 7 research agents that cover technical analysis, fundamentals and news sentiment.
- **Smart Money.** A view of insider, congressional and "guru" activity.
- **Portfolio tools.** Factor analysis, optimization and guru portfolios on Premium.

Kavout is honest about what the picker is. Its own page calls it a screening tool, and its picks "a starting point for research". Every one of these tells you where to look. None says why.

We read Kavout's pages and didn't test its scores or its agents. This is what its pages say, not how it performs.

- **A low price for a trader.** Pro is $16 a month billed yearly.

## What does a score leave you to find out?

A score is a good place to start. It's a bad place to stop.

Say a stock reads Outperform. You still don't know who pays the company, whether management keeps its word, or what the shares could be worth if things go well or badly. Here is what one real report answers.

**VICI Properties owns casino resorts and rents them out.** Our [VICI report](/stocks/vici/) says Caesars and MGM supply about 74% of its leasing revenue. In the third quarter of 2026, profit per share fell to $0.31 from $0.71 a year earlier. Over the same quarter, AFFO, the cash measure property trusts use, rose to $0.62 from $0.60. Behind the drop: a $458 million credit-loss allowance, $313.2 million of it tied to Caesars. Rent was still being paid.

Two numbers pointing opposite ways. Which one matters? A score doesn't say. The report does, in plain words.

Then it asks the next question: what could the shares be worth? VICI's forecast has three cases with their chances: bear 27%, base 55%, bull 18%. The probability-weighted price is $24.76 in October 2027, against $23.52 on 29 October, a 13% return with dividends. No buy or sell call. You decide.
