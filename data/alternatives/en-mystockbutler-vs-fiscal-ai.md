## Can you use both?

Yes. A sensible order:

1. Find and screen in the terminal.
2. When a name survives, read the report instead of building the case yourself.
3. Open the financial statements to check the numbers your decision rests on.

## Questions

### Which is better, Fiscal.ai or MyStockButler?

They do different jobs. Fiscal.ai is a data terminal with an API. MyStockButler writes a full report on one company. If you want data to research with, Fiscal.ai. If you want the research done and written down, MyStockButler.

### Is MyStockButler a Fiscal.ai alternative?

For the job of understanding one company, yes: it gives you the analysis instead of the raw data. For screening, building your own models or pulling data into your own tools, it is not.

### Does Fiscal.ai write reports?

On the pages we read, Fiscal.ai offers data, tools, an API and connectors for AI assistants. We found no written report on a single company.

### Does MyStockButler show where its numbers come from?

Yes. Every number is traced to its source, and the report lists every source: Amazon's lists 230. Open the filing behind any figure and read it.

### How do I know MyStockButler's numbers are right?

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed. A final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI models write the reports, so one can still get a number wrong: check the numbers you rely on.

### Is MyStockButler investment advice?

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
