## What do you get after the screen?

A screen answers the question you thought to ask. A report answers the ones you didn't. Here is the difference on a real report, [Coca-Cola's](/stocks/ko/).

### "My screen found Coca-Cola. Why does it earn so much?"

Coca-Cola kept **28.7 cents of operating profit from each dollar** of sales in 2025: $13,762 million on $47,941 million of revenue. Its own bottling business earned **$426 million of operating income on $5,735 million of revenue**, about 7 cents per dollar. That is one reason Coca-Cola sold most of its bottlers and kept the high-margin part, the concentrate. A metric column shows you the margin. The report shows you where it comes from.

### "The yield looks fine. Does the cash back it up?"

Free cash flow was $5,296 million in 2025. Take off $279 million of stock pay, the shares given to employees, and real free cash flow is $5,017 million. We count it that way for every company. For [Alphabet](/stocks/googl/) the same step takes $73,266 million of free cash flow down to $48,313 million.

### "What could go wrong from here?"

The report prices three cases. For Coca-Cola, bear 25%, base 55%, bull 20%. By October 2029 the shares are worth $54.72, $83.68 or $116.10 in those cases, and the probability-weighted price is $82.93, a 2% loss including dividends against $91.18 on 28 October 2026. The report says what the price hinges on: volume, cash generation, and valuation overcoming the Africa headwind. It makes no buy or sell call.

### "A bank doesn't work like a soft-drink company."

We use a different set of tables for each of ten types of company, including banks, insurers, property trusts, oil companies and miners. A bank's report tracks interest margin, capital and loan losses. [Realty Income's](/stocks/o/) forecast runs on AFFO, the cash measure property trusts use.

## Questions

### Is Stock Rover free?

Partly. There is a limited free plan and a 14-day trial with every feature and no credit card. Paid plans run from $34 to $199 a month.

### Is Stock Rover worth it?

For screening and portfolio tracking, yes: Premium is $34 a month and the trial is free. For a decision on one company, you still do the reading. A report that traces each number to its source does that reading for you.

### Does Stock Rover have earnings call transcripts?

Its own review page says "No earnings-call transcripts". MyStockButler's research hub holds the last 8 earnings-call transcripts for a covered company, in full text.

### What is a good alternative to Stock Rover?

For a terminal with charts and transcripts, Koyfin. For global financials and a valuation model, TIKR. For full written reports with every number traced to its source, MyStockButler (our product): your first S&P 500 report is free, and $30 a month opens every S&P 500 report.

### How do I know MyStockButler's numbers are right?

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed. A final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI models write the reports, so one can still get a number wrong: check the numbers you rely on.

### Is MyStockButler investment advice?

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
