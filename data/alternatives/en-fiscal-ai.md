## What does Fiscal.ai give you?

- **The Terminal.** Its Terminal page, read on 29 October 2026, calls it an "all-in-one research platform" with "global data across financials linked to source, estimates, transcripts, IR content, filings, news, ownership and more". It has plans for individual investors and a 7-day free trial.
- **The API.** Its documentation, read on 2 November 2026, lists coverage of the USA, Canada, US ADRs, the UK and the EU, with 20+ annual and 40+ quarterly periods of financials and ratios, stock prices back 30+ years, insider Form 4 and institutional 13F ownership, and events with transcripts and audio.
- **Source links.** In the API, "Sourcing to Filing" is an add-on that links each data point to an image and a PDF of the source table.
- **Connectors.** A Claude connector, a ChatGPT app and an MCP endpoint, so an AI assistant can pull its data. The API key page starts a free trial with no credit card.
- **Price.** On 2 November 2026 Fiscal.ai's site asked our reader for a browser check, so we can't quote its paid prices. Open its pricing page before you decide.

**What Fiscal.ai does well:** a wide data set with a trail back to the filing, and tools for people who build their own research.

**What it leaves to you:** everything that needs a person to read. On the pages we read, Fiscal.ai sells data, tools and connectors. We found no written report on a single company. You decide what to look at, and what it means.

## What does a report add to linked data?

A terminal answers the question you thought to ask. A report answers the ones you didn't. Here is the difference on a real report, [Amazon's](/stocks/amzn/).

### "Free cash flow is positive. Is Amazon making money?"

Amazon's cash from operations in 2025 was $139,514 million. It spent $131,819 million on capital. Free cash flow: $7,695 million. Now take off the shares it gave employees as pay, $19,467 million, and real free cash flow is minus $11,772 million. A table shows you each line. The report adds them up, says why, and says what it hinges on: whether the AWS investment turns into durable cash.

### "What is it worth if I'm wrong?"

Three cases, with probabilities: bear 30%, base 50%, bull 20%. By October 2029 the shares are worth $228.60, $488.62 or $733.98, and the weighted price is $459.69, against $243.79 on 29 October 2026. You see the range, and what moves it. No buy or sell call.

### "Where do the numbers come from?"

Amazon's report lists 230 sources. 137 of them, 60%, are the company's own site or a regulator. Every number traced to its source: click, read the filing, decide.

### "Did management keep its promises?"

Where management gives forecasts, we check them against results and score the record out of 5. Amazon scores 5 of 5: net sales beat management's range in 6 quarters of 7, operating income in 5 of 7.

### "I use Claude with a data connector. What more do I need?"

A connector gives an assistant the data. You still write the questions: what to ask, in what order, about which risks. Our report is those questions, answered first. The same eight chapters for every company: the business, its market, history and capital decisions, management and ownership, the numbers, the valuation and the stock, what comes next, and where the price could go.

## Questions

### Is Fiscal.ai free?

Not fully. The Terminal has a 7-day free trial, and its API key page starts a free trial with no credit card. For paid prices, check Fiscal.ai's pricing page.

### Is Fiscal.ai worth it?

If you build your own research and want global data with a link back to the filing, it can be. If you want the case on one company written and checked for you, a report does that job instead.

### Does Fiscal.ai link every number to its source?

Its Terminal page says its financials are "linked to source", and its API has a "Sourcing to Filing" add-on that links each data point to an image and PDF of the source table. MyStockButler traces every number in a report to its source and lists every source.

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed. A final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI models write the reports, so one can still get a number wrong: check the numbers you rely on.

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
