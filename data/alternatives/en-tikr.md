# TIKR review 2026: price, what you get, and alternatives

TIKR is a data terminal for global stocks: up to 30 years of financials, estimates, transcripts and a valuation model, free to $119.95 a month. It hands you the data and leaves the thinking to you. MyStockButler writes the analysis: one long report per company, every number traced to its source. Your first S&P 500 report is free.

TIKR says it covers "100,000+ stocks across 92 countries and 136 exchanges", with data "powered by S&P Global CapitalIQ". If you want the raw material to build your own model, that is a lot of material. But a model is only as good as the assumptions you type into it. Which growth rate? Which multiple? Our report gives you three cases with probabilities, and shows the numbers behind each. Type a ticker below and sign up, no card. TIKR backs its plans with a 14-day money-back guarantee. Its home page says it tracks the holdings of "10,000+ investors".

**What TIKR does well:** global coverage, a free plan that needs no card, and a long transcript archive on the top plans. The entry price for global data is $24.95 a month.

**What it leaves to you:** the thinking. You read the transcripts, you set the growth rate, you pick the multiple, you decide what the answer means. On the pages we read, TIKR sells data and tools. We found no written report on a company.

## What does a report add to the data?

### "I filled in the model. What could go wrong?"

Our [TSMC report](/stocks/tsm/) builds the model for you and shows its work. It gives three cases, with probabilities: bear 25%, base 55%, bull 20%. In October 2027 the bear case is $316.21 a share, the base $484.67, the bull $613.62. The weighted price is $468.34, a 15% return including dividends against $412.61 at the time of the report. It then says what has to go right: leading-edge capacity running well loaded, and AI demand turning into orders.

It makes no buy or sell call. You see the range, and what moves it.

### "Where did this number come from?"

TSMC's report cites 407 sources. 335 of them, 82%, are the company's own site or a regulator. Every number traced to its source: click, read the filing, decide.

### "Who actually runs this company?"

Beside the report sits a research hub. For TSMC it holds bios for all 7 executives and 22 interview and appearance links covering 6 of them, each checked to be the right person. CFO Wendell Huang, for one, is there with a Yahoo Finance video from October 2026. It also holds the last 8 earnings-call transcripts in full text, and insider tables.

TIKR keeps a deeper transcript archive on its top plans. The hub puts management's own words next to the report that tests them.

### "Is this company's record any good?"

Where management gives forecasts, we check them against results and score the record out of 5. VICI scores 4 of 5. See [VICI](/stocks/vici/).

## Questions

### Is TIKR free?

Partly. The free plan covers U.S. stocks with no credit card: 5 years of financials, 8 quarters, and 90 days of transcripts. Global coverage starts with Plus at $24.95 a month.

### Is TIKR worth it?

If you build your own valuation models, yes: Plus is $24.95 a month for global data and Ultimate goes back 30 years. If you want someone to read the company and write up the case, a report does that job instead.

### Where does TIKR get its data?

TIKR's home page says its data is "powered by S&P Global CapitalIQ".

### What is a good alternative to TIKR?

For screening and charting, Koyfin. For portfolios and dividends, Stock Rover. For full written reports with every number traced to its source, MyStockButler (our product): your first S&P 500 report is free, and $30 a month opens every S&P 500 report.

### How do I know MyStockButler's numbers are right?

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed. A final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI models write the reports, so one can still get a number wrong: check the numbers you rely on.

### Is MyStockButler investment advice?

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
