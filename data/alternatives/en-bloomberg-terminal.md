# Bloomberg Terminal alternatives for individual investors

For an individual investor researching a stock, the best Bloomberg Terminal alternatives cost from nothing to about $40 a month: Stock Analysis and Yahoo Finance for free numbers, Morningstar or Seeking Alpha for analysts' opinions, and a full report on one company from MyStockButler (our product). The Terminal itself is built for professionals who trade all day, with live prices on every market, news, chat and data on bonds and derivatives. Bloomberg does not publish its price; third-party estimates for 2025 and 2026 range from about $27,000 to $32,000 a year per seat.

## The short answer

- **Free:** Stock Analysis and Yahoo Finance give you five or more years of financial statements, ratios and news on almost any listed company.
- **Full written reports:** MyStockButler (our product) has reports on 685 companies, every number linked to the document it comes from; the first one is free, and $30 a month opens every S&P 500 report.
- **Analyst opinions:** Morningstar Investor (fair value and analyst reports) and Seeking Alpha Premium (articles and ratings).
- **Screening many stocks quickly:** Simply Wall St (visual scores) and Stock Analysis.

## What you give up without a Terminal

Being honest about it: none of these tools replaces a Terminal for a professional. You give up live prices on every asset class, instant news, the chat network, bond and derivatives data, and trading from the same screen. If your job depends on those, the Terminal is worth its price.

For an individual investor buying a few companies a year, those features matter less than reading the company correctly: what it sells, how it makes money, how much cash it really produces, what management promised and delivered, and what the current price already assumes.

## Questions

### Is there a free alternative to the Bloomberg Terminal?

For researching a company, yes. Stock Analysis and Yahoo Finance show financial statements, ratios and news for free. For live professional data on every market, no free tool comes close.

### How much does a Bloomberg Terminal cost?

Bloomberg does not publish its price. Third-party estimates for 2025 and 2026 range from about $27,000 to $32,000 a year per seat, roughly $2,300 to $2,700 a month.

### Can an individual investor get a Bloomberg Terminal?

Bloomberg sells the Terminal on contract; ask Bloomberg whether it will contract with an individual. Most individual investors do not need one, because they use a fraction of what it does.

### What is the cheapest way to research one stock?

Free options exist: Stock Analysis and Yahoo Finance show the numbers, and Simply Wall St gives 5 company reports a month.
