## From the picture to the reasoning

A score tells you where to look. Here is what you get when you look closer, from our real reports.

### "It looks good. But how does this business really make money?"

Our [Coca-Cola report](/stocks/ko/) walks you through it. Coca-Cola kept **28.7 cents of operating profit from each dollar** of sales in 2025: $13,762 million on $47,941 million of revenue. Its own bottling business earned **$426 million of operating income on $5,735 million of revenue**, about 7 cents per dollar: one reason Coca-Cola sold most of its bottlers and kept the high-margin part, the concentrate.

### "I've never heard of this company."

The reports are written for you. Terms are explained as they come up and a line under each table says what it means. The same report tells you that the company creates demand for the brand, "while a network of bottlers often makes, packages and delivers the drink". Any report also reads in French, German or Chinese (Hong Kong), on a click, in about 5 minutes.

### "Profit looks great. Where's the cash?"

Free cash flow leaves out stock-based pay, the shares given to employees. We count it. [Alphabet's](/stocks/googl/) 2025 free cash flow of $73,266 million drops to $48,313 million once you take off $24,953 million of stock pay.

### "A bank doesn't work like a soft-drink company."

We use a different set of tables for each of ten types of company, including banks, insurers, property trusts, oil companies and miners. A bank's report tracks interest margin, capital and loan losses. [Realty Income's](/stocks/o/) forecast runs on AFFO, the cash measure property trusts use.

### "Did management do what it said?"

Where management gives forecasts, we check them against results and score the record out of 5. [First Solar's](/stocks/fslr/earnings/) original 2025 forecast was earnings of $17 to $20 a share. It delivered $14.21, and scores 1 of 5.

### "The CEO sold $17.5 million of stock. Should I worry?"

You get the trade and what it means, in plain words. McDonald's CEO sold 52,553 shares for about $17.5 million gross in February, under a pre-arranged trading plan. Our report reads it as planned personal selling, "not a stand-alone warning".

### "Results came out this morning. Is this already out of date?"

The 100 largest S&P 500 companies and the day's most talked-about stocks are redone first when they report, usually within about 5 hours.

### "How do I know the numbers are right?"

Every number is traced to its source and every source is listed: 219 in the Coca-Cola report. AI models write the report, inside our own pipeline that builds the tables and checks the numbers, and a final check corrects or deletes statements it finds untrue. AI can still get a number wrong, so check the ones you rely on.

## Questions

Partly. The free plan shows 5 company reports a month. Premium costs $10.95 a month and Unlimited $21.50 a month, both billed yearly.

For screening many stocks, yes: the free plan covers 5 company reports a month and Premium is $10.95 a month, billed yearly. For a decision on one company, read the key numbers in its own annual report, or in a report that traces each number to its source.

### Is Simply Wall St accurate?

Treat its scores as a first look. Before a decision, check the key numbers in the company's own annual report.

### How do I know MyStockButler's numbers are right?

No. Our reports present facts, numbers and scenarios with their sources. They never tell you to buy or sell.
