# Simply Wall St review and alternatives

Simply Wall St is good value for screening many stocks: its free plan shows 5 company reports a month, and its visual "Snowflake" sums up valuation, growth, past performance, health and dividends in one picture. Its limit is depth: a score summarises, while a report shows the filing behind each number. If you want to understand one company before buying it, the alternatives below go deeper or cost nothing.

## What Simply Wall St costs and gives you

Prices as shown on Simply Wall St's pricing page on 26 October 2026, in US dollars.

- **Free:** 5 company reports a month.
- **Premium:** $10.95 a month, billed yearly ($131.40), 30 company reports a month.
- **Unlimited:** $21.50 a month, billed yearly ($258).
- **What you get:** the Snowflake score, a valuation model, risks and rewards in short lines, portfolio tracking, an AI assistant.
- **Good at:** comparing many companies quickly; clear visuals; a generous free plan.
- **Keep in mind:** the analysis is presented as scores and short text. Check the numbers you rely on against the company's own reports.

## A score or a report: an example on Coca-Cola

A score summarises; a report shows the filing behind each number. Our [Coca-Cola report](/stocks/ko/), from the company's 2025 filings:

- Coca-Cola kept **28.7 cents of operating profit from each dollar** of sales in 2025: $13,762 million on $47,941 million of revenue.
- Its own bottling business earned **$426 million of operating income on $5,735 million of revenue**, about 7 cents per dollar: one reason Coca-Cola sold most of its bottlers and kept the high-margin part, the concentrate.

## Questions

### Is Simply Wall St free?

Partly. The free plan shows 5 company reports a month. Premium costs $10.95 a month and Unlimited $21.50 a month, both billed yearly.

### Is Simply Wall St worth it?

For screening many stocks, yes: the free plan covers 5 company reports a month and Premium is $10.95 a month, billed yearly. For a decision on one company, read the key numbers in its own annual report, or in a report that links each number to it.

### Is Simply Wall St accurate?

Treat its scores as a first look. Before a decision, check the key numbers in the company's own annual report.
