# Corrections

If you find a wrong number or a wrong statement in one of our reports or pages, please tell us.

## How to report an error

Write to [mystockbutler@gmail.com](mailto:mystockbutler@gmail.com). Name the company, the page or chapter, and the number or sentence you think is wrong. If you can, add the document that shows the right one.

## What we do with it

We check it against the company's own documents. If it is wrong, we fix the number or remove it, and we redo the report where needed.

## When a page changes its date

A company page shows the date of the report it quotes. When the report is redone, the page takes the new report's date. Our guides show the date of their last full update. A small fix on one of these pages leaves its date as it was.
